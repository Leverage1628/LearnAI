Execution Rules for the Rebuild
01
Percentages, not dollar amounts. This plan works at $67, $167, or $1,670. Sizing by share of book keeps it valid as capital changes.
02
Four positions sized well beat twelve sized poorly. The fragmentation flaw solved itself in the liquidation — don't rebuild it. If a name can't justify 5%+, it doesn't enter yet.
03
Stage order is the point. Don't skip to Stage 4 because a headline is exciting. Anchor first, upgrade quality second, fill the empty tier third, extend last.
04
Verify before buying anything in Stage 3. AFK's expense ratio is reported inconsistently and it's a small fund — check the fund page directly. Wide spreads eat small positions.
05
Two clocks, checked separately. CDE + GOLD + AU are all Clock A (gold, rates, dollar). FCX + UUUU + COPX are Clock B (growth, China, buildout). It's easy to accidentally triple the same bet across three tickers.
06
Watch rates as closely as policy. 10Y at 4.69%, cooler CPI/PPI pushing hikes out. UUUU is the most rate-sensitive name in the plan — a hawkish turn hurts it more than a China deal would.
07
Still not a hedge. Everything above is equity. In a liquidity event these correlate toward 1 regardless of underlying commodity. A non-equity sleeve (CPER, physical gold, PDBC) is the eventual fix — probably not yet practical at current size.
